# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Console operations

- **Set animal as on display** suggests only species that are off display now or in the future, and lists only exhibits that have at least one such animal. Choosing an exhibit narrows the species list. **Set animal as off display** still uses the full catalog.
- **Remove visibility schedule** suggests only species that have a visibility schedule now or in the future, and lists only exhibits that have at least one such animal. Choosing an exhibit narrows the species list. **Set animal visibility schedule** still uses the full catalog.
- **Remove animal viewing alert** suggests only species that have a viewing alert now or in the future, and lists only exhibits that have at least one such animal. Choosing an exhibit narrows the species list. **Set animal viewing alert** still uses the full catalog.
- **Set exhibit as open** lists only exhibits that are closed now or in the future. **Set exhibit as closed** still uses the full catalog.
- **Set restroom as open** lists only restrooms that are closed now or in the future. **Set restroom as closed** still uses the full catalog.
- **Remove restroom alert** lists only restrooms that have an alert now or in the future. **Set restroom alert** still uses the full catalog.
- **Set transportation station as open** lists only stations that are closed now or in the future. **Set transportation station as closed** still uses the full catalog.
